# AI use disclosure

OpenAI Codex provided substantial assistance with source research, acquisition,
topic modeling, sentiment analysis, statistical code, tests, the executed
notebook, report, figures and documentation. The student supplied the assignment
and prior-project preferences. Codex read the previous four NLP assignment chats
and local projects to preserve submission conventions.

Parallel agents assisted with news/social and market acquisition, public polling
and company-source research, historical context, scientific-method tests,
measurement review and sentiment-model evaluation. The frozen 60-text review
was labeled by an AI agent before scores were viewed. Eight records subsequently
fell outside the final eligible corpus after timing/relevance screens; contextual/VADER comparison uses the
same 52 eligible records. This is an AI-reference development diagnostic, not
independent human labeling or verified student validation.

The initial topic model isolated repeated quotations from a single HN thread.
Training-text-only support diagnostics motivated minimum document frequency
five and concentration screens. Initial lexical sentiment diagnostics exposed
sarcasm, quotation and target errors, motivating comparison with an off-the-shelf,
immutably pinned contextual model. No model was fine-tuned on election prices,
equity returns or the review labels. Model choice consulted the development
diagnostic, so that diagnostic is not presented as an untouched accuracy test.
Topic selection and source screens were developed retrospectively; the later
window assesses a frozen text model, not a prospectively registered strategy.

All empirical results derive from retrieved observations. Sources, payload
hashes, retrieval/publication clocks, query caps, platform failures, exclusions,
model artifacts and missing observations are recorded locally. Synthetic
fixtures are confined to software tests. Neither prices nor missing sentiment
were invented, and unavailable APIs are not represented as acquired corpora.
The October voter survey is interpretation only and never backfilled into
historical signals.

Wording valence is not party support, voter intention or validated population
sentiment. Company policy signs are conditional hypotheses, and no causal
election effect or profitable out-of-sample result is claimed. Dataset exclusion
follows the user's prior repository policy. Codex prepared and uploaded the
requested repository; no trade or institutional submission was made.
